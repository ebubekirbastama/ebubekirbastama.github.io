Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EBS Elite PDF handles information when you use the application.

Effective date: July 28, 2026 Package: com.ebs.elitepdf

1. Overview

EBS Elite PDF is a PDF reading and document management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your documents responsibly.

The application is designed to process PDF documents, images, and related files primarily on your device.

2. Information We Collect

EBS Elite PDF does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or payment information.

The application may access the following data only when necessary to provide its features:

  • PDF files, images, and documents selected by you.
  • Camera access when you use document scanning or OCR-related features.
  • Device storage or media access when you open, save, export, or manage files.
  • Basic technical information automatically provided by the Android operating system.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information accessed by the application is used only to:

  • Open and display PDF documents.
  • Edit, merge, split, compress, convert, or organize documents.
  • Scan documents and recognize text using OCR features.
  • Save files to a location selected by you.
  • Share files when you explicitly choose a sharing option.
  • Improve application stability, compatibility, and performance.

4. Local Processing and File Storage

Documents are processed locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to share, export, open, or transfer them through another application or service.

EBS Elite PDF does not automatically upload your documents to a remote server.

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy practices of any third-party service you choose when sharing or exporting a document.

5. Permissions

The application may request permissions such as:

  • Camera: Used for document scanning and image capture.
  • Photos, media, or files: Used to open, save, edit, and organize documents.
  • Notifications: Used only if the application provides document or task notifications.

Permissions are used only for the features you request. You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on Android system components or third-party libraries for functions such as PDF rendering, OCR, file selection, crash reporting, or performance monitoring.

If future versions include analytics, advertising, cloud storage, or other online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those services and the information they may process.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Files are shared only when you deliberately use a sharing, export, print, cloud, email, messaging, or “open with” function.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

EBS Elite PDF is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not require account registration, it does not intentionally request age-related or identity information.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • Choose which documents the application can access.
  • Revoke camera, storage, media, or notification permissions.
  • Delete files created or stored by the application.
  • Uninstall the application to remove its locally stored application data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, permissions, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
